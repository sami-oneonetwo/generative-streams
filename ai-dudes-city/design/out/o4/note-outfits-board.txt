Design review, 24 Oct 2026: crisp, playful, consistent?
Crisp: in four ages of six. The kits are chunky and every age has its own silhouettes (hides and spears, linen and jars, hoods and aprons, caps and a top hat), but the Stone and Wasteland rows are brown people on brown ground and lose their edges at phone size, and the Overseer melts into his own townsfolk. Playful: yes: the antler headdress, the jester's motley, the Alderman's top hat and chain, the tour guide's flag. Consistent: the step from age to age holds, but the head breaks the side colours (the King's cape is red; the Chief and the Overseer have no gold to say who they are), and the caravan leader is a different stranger in every age, where the story needs one band. Astra's critique: revise, 6/10 (critique-outfits-board.json). The red on some everyday people is the sample chatter's own shirt, which the kits take on.
