## 2. Biggest game wins

**The age turn needs a moment** ×2. 1.5–2 h of work lands as one `setAge` tick: travellers vanish mid-confetti, the next caravan teleports to the gate and opens visit 1 at once. Add an `ageTurn` scene (head cuts the ribbon at the Wonder, a ring sweeps out and swaps builds as it passes, old caravan waves off, title card "THE BRONZE AGE", top 3 contributors named, 60–90 s breather, then the next caravan walks in). `caravan.ts:388-393`, `city.ts:95-105`, `shared/scenes.ts:66`. **M–L**

**The run softlocks around age 4–5** ×2. The square has 4 community lots, finished civics never leave, a full run needs 6 Wonders, and chat projects share the same lots → "the square has no room left" and the age can't turn without admin. Also *any* finished project satisfies the Wonder ask (a hot-dog stand can end the Stone Age). Give the Wonder its own spot (fountain/plaza) that each age replaces or relocates as a relic; tag the Wonder project when the caravan/leader proposes it and only that one completes the ask. `shared/city.ts:96-98`, `city.ts:606-615,832`, `caravan.ts:129`. **S–M**

**Every age is the same three visits with renamed nouns** ×2. Feed travellers → workshop/shifts/party → any project. Wonder cost uses the generic `80+25·awake` formula, so the Scrap Rocket costs what the Furnace did. Make asks data-driven per age with one signature ask each (Bronze river raiders/defend, Medieval feast with heckling jester, Industrial rail line…), use the doc's Supply/Occasion/Commission kinds, scale Wonder cost by age, give it an Operate milestone ("forge 10 tools", "fuel the rocket"). Also count existing/upgraded places toward "build N eateries" so the 32-lot town doesn't fill. `caravan.ts:110-139,432-436`, `city.ts:624-626`. **M**

**Show the age on stream.** Board says only `${name} · visit N of 3`; no stream client reads `ageStyle`. Add "Age 3/6 · The Middle Ages" + a thin six-step strip. `client/dudes/main.ts:838`. **S**

**Age 6 ends in silence and the reset wipes all history.** Finale scene (rocket launch, roll call of each age's heroes, daughter's last line) and something that survives reset: hall of heroes / relic plaques / chatters keep their dude & look / "Run 2" counter. `caravan.ts:382-388`, `crier.ts:247`, `state.ts:790-794`. **M**

**Restyles don't transform convincingly.** They only start at the turn (no one-ahead), run one at a time behind chatter designs (30+ min of palette-swap on a busy night), pop in silently, and the prompt is cut at 300 chars so "keep its layout" is always lost (~414 chars). Wonders get restyled too. Start in visit 3, scaffold-and-pop, shorten prompt, exclude Wonders. `index.ts:763-790` (`:784`), `mayor.ts:405-419`. **M**

**Relics aren't built** — cheapest strong cross-stream hook (chat races to name a favourite build; it stays). `docs/ages.md:45-58`. Also fix `docs/ages.md:3` ("Nothing here is built yet"). **M**

**Later caravans just appear; the daughter goes quiet.** Short arrival beat per age reusing the opening's shot language; one daughter line per age turn; make her the Wasteland's Road Boss. `caravan.ts:85-88,102-103,148`. **S–M**

## 6. Polish & ops

- **Geometry cache can show the wrong building after a crash** (ids/revisions reused after a 15 s-autosave rollback). Per-boot epoch in `hello` → clear cache. `objects.ts:253-290`. **S**
- **Effects uncapped, one material per spark** — 10 armed players can tank the 30 fps OBS feed; server trims to 60 so some hits never render. Cap ~300 meshes, share materials, raise/merge server cap. `effects.ts:70,102-140,329`, `sim.ts:695`. **S**
- **/admin gaps**: send dude home, clear a bubble/line, cancel in-flight designs / empty job queue, spend readout. `data/log/terminal.jsonl` grows forever and is read whole each hot reload — rotate it. `index.ts:1862`. **M**
- **Tests missing on the riskiest code**: `loadState`/`saveState` refuse-to-boot paths, `PlaySeats`, `WsHub` backpressure, `chatCompletion` retry/timeout, /play say vs mute, restyle fan-out. Cheapest win: parse a checked-in copy of the live save against `stateSchema` so a schema bump can't kill the stream on hot reload. **M**

## What's working — protect it

- Chatter's own words go straight into the bubble while their agent decides the body; off-menu ideas become "bits". Strong identity, near-zero friction.
- Needs loop is model-free, favours other people's places, floats `+§5` to owners; "nothing bad while away" + welcome-back summary.
- Stone Age opening (stranded band, bridge mended by a share of chat, crossing scene) is a real set piece — the template for every age turn.
- Crier is template-only; chat text is never built into HTML; Listener/design prompts treat chat as untrusted.
- Art pipeline: all six ages have full, even piece sets; calm ground; per-age outfits.
- Ops: atomic saves with `.bak`, refuses to overwrite unreadable data, AbortController-guarded AI calls, 2 MB backpressure drop, capped per-dude lists, server-side weapon cooldowns, HMAC-checked play cookies, Worker path allowlist, admin guard rejects forwarded requests.
