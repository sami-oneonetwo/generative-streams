## Why (what changes for viewers or Sami)

Agents can't hear, so they need numbers: is it clipping, is it louder than the voices, does it vanish on a phone? Sami needs one table that says what every sound is, where it fires and how loud it is.

## Scope

- New scripts/audio-render.mjs: bundles audio.ts with esbuild (already a devDependency) into a small render page, opens it with playwright-core and the Chrome from ~/gs-tools (as shot.mjs does, so no new npm dependency) and renders, through OfflineAudioContext with a seeded random: every cue, a 5-line voice set, a 15-talker crowd and 32 bars of the town tune, as WAVs under /tmp/fk-audio/<yyyymmdd-hhmm>/.
- For each render: peak, true peak (4× oversampled), RMS, approximate LUFS (K-weighted, gated), loudness in LU against the voice set (the blips are 0 LU), the share of energy under 150 Hz, the clip count, and a silence check (exact zeros where a cue says silence). All of it goes in levels.json beside the WAVs.
- New scripts/audio-check.mjs: fails on any clip, a true peak above −1 dBTP, or a level outside its target against the blips: music −10 to −12 LU, UI −8 LU, ambience −14 LU, fanfare at most +3 LU (one table at the top, from board/audio-plan.md §3). `npm run audio:check` is one script line in package.json, which the coordinator adds; until then run `node scripts/audio-check.mjs`.
- New docs/audio-levels.md, generated by the check: one row per cue with its bus, trigger (file:line), peak, LU against the blips, rate limit, whether it may play in scenes, its share under 150 Hz, and the date Sami approved it (from audio-approved.json).
- The baseline: today's sounds, measured, committed in docs/audio-levels.md. Anything already off target (boom's sub-only content, for one) is listed as known, not failed, and goes to FK-172.
- Runs under `nice -n 10` and behind `/tmp/fk-shot.lock`, because it drives Chrome like a screenshot.
