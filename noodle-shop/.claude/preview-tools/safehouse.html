A neighborhood, not a toy island. Roofless rooms, a scrappy vegetable garden, rain barrels, salvaged timber, and a fortified perimeter. The camera stays high enough to read the whole safehouse while keeping the survivor visible.

Original procedural artwork, informed by Project Zomboid. No game assets reused. Day/night and subtle character motion are preview controls only; building, zombies, and chat are not live systems.
