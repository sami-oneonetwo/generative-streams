The direction I would try first

DYSMANTLE's readable color + Zomboid's neighborhood framing + Aftermath's pools of light. A little survivor building up a safehouse, with abandoned streets and zombies beyond the fence. Keep the place readable in daylight; let warm windows and campfires explain where safety is at night. Borrow the visual principles, not the games' assets or heavy survival systems.
